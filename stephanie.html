Stephanie Joy

Fighting For A Fair & Just Electorial System

People have called me to ask me to continue my campaign for Cook County Clerk. People need a formidable candidate that is qualified, credentialed and is willing to diligently to get the job done.

People living in our nation, state and in Cook County, IL are sick and tired of the same ol’ recycled politicians that run for different offices, in order to keep the same ol’ status quo, power and political favors.

I played their version of the reality competition TV show “Wipe Out.”, which only in this case should be called “Kicked-Off” similar in that contestants try to navigate an extreme obstacle course that has been designed to provoke an unprecedented number of crashes, face plants and wipeouts, with one competitor taking home a $50,000 prize, During the Primary Election, the Democratic Party candidates file and try to navigate extreme election laws that has been designed to provoke an unprecedented number of filing errors, objections and withdraws with the intent of having one candidate remaining on the ballot at the time of the primary election.

Stephanie Joy made her decision to continue her campaign for Cook County Clerk as a Write-In Candidate for the March 20, 2018 Primary Election, after discussing the matter with her family and receiving positive feedback and resounding support from family and friends.

On February 1, 2018, the Cook County Electoral Board, sustained an objection made by a man named Mr. Featherson, living in Maywood, IL hired by Karen Yarbrough, who randomly and deliberately objected to 4,311 signatures in an effort to knock her off the ballot for the upcoming Primary election.

Stephanie Joy Jackson-Rowe’s filed 9,707 signatures on December 4, 2017. The required number of signatures is 8,236 to run for Cook County Clerk.

Strictly a numbers game tactic. While going through the Record Examination, it became apparent that the objector haphazardly marked blank lines in bad faith. He objected to blank lines and pages of signatures stating that they were duplicates, which proved valid. He even marked every signature on a petition sheet signed by members of Stephanie’s church. All of the signatures once checked were found to be registered voters. Stephanie informed her Pastor and church members of what had occurred. This was wrong, bogus and apparently is a tactic used to minimize the number of candidate names that appear on the ballot. The Democratic Machine would like their battered recycled candidate to run unopposed and that is why they objected.

The Green Party candidate Jan Kowalski McDonald, also hired a person to file an objection against Stephanie Joy’s filed signatures. The Electoral Board overruled the objection in January 2018. Mr. Featherstone objected to Jan McDonald appearing on the ballot. Ms. McDonald has been kick-off too.

For the record, Stephanie Joy did not object to either candidate running for Cook County Clerk. David Orr is retiring after serving for more than 20 years, and it is important that people have a choice to decide who they want to represent the Democratic Party in the General Election in November of 2018. This should not be decided by the Democratic Machine, because they want to continue recycle embattled politicians, even those under investigation. This is wrong. The status quo is broken!

Currently, only one name will appear on the ballot and that is why I am running as a Write-In Candidate. Someone must stand up against the Democratic Machine, giving people a choice during the Primary election.
