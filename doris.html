Doris Davenport

Fighting For A Fair & Just Electorial System

Consultant/Civic Leader/Author/Talk Show Host “Doris Davenport is a civic-minded leader with a results oriented background in community and economic development, strategic marketing and public relations. This lifelong learner loves faith, family, civics, tennis and inspiring others.

Doris joins the Ballot Democracy team with a consistent dedication to civic-minded servant leadership.

Doris has earned the nickname, “Change Agent, for the thoughtful courage, energy and tenacity she brings to projects.” Her leadership skills demonstrate a strong track record of performance in high paced organizations. She utilizes keen analysis, insights, superior interpersonal skills and a team approach to facilitate partnerships and drive implementation of strategies and processes—positioning clients to win. She is adept at working in political, business and multicultural community environments.

Doris has participated in major corporate and government contract negotiations and international labor union training, directed a 500-plus member business organization, co-lead a team publishing an international lifestyle publication, lead the marketing and sales team for a major IT company, managed marketing and public relations for the largest U.S. Department of Commerce minority business development program, partnered with legal experts conducting a third party review of major U.S. auto manufacturing companies and their practices with minority dealerships. She is known for her work in political strategy and community organizing.

Doris hosts the fast growing local talk radio program, The Doris Davenport Show “All Local All The Time” on WPNA 1490AM and heard globally on the world wide web. She is also a visiting guest host on The Ben Joravsky Show.

Doris earned a master’s degree in Theological Studies from McCormick Theological Seminary with work toward an MDIV at Moody Theological Seminary. President William Jefferson Clinton and Vice President Al Gore honored Doris Davenport for her work in small business development when she represented the State of Illinois at the White House Conference for Small Business, an honor she earned in a statewide election process.

Civic Boards Served - Chicago Sister Cities, Children’s Place Association, SOS Children’s Village, Kohl Children’s Museum

Gubernatorial appointments - Pension Modernization Task Force Commissioner, University of Illinois Admissions Review Commission Commissioner—Served with Judge Abner Mikva

Non-Profit Service - One Earth Film Festival, Oak Park Climate Action Committee, Chicago Symphony Orchestra African American Network, Chicago West Community Music Center

Executive Leadership - The Foster Group, African American Leadership Partnership, Rainbow Push Coalition, Burgos & Assoc., (U.S. Department of Commerce, MEGA Center)
